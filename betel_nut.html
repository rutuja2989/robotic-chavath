Betelnut in Ganpati Matoli

Spiritual Representation

In Goa, during the celebration of Ganesh Chaturthi, the Matoli canopy is decorated with a variety of fruits, vegetables, herbs, and sacred plants. Among these, the betelnut holds a very special place.

Betelnut, also known as Supari, is regarded as a symbol of auspiciousness, strength, and purity in Hindu tradition. It is often offered during rituals and pujas as a sacred item representing prosperity and respect to the deities.

In the Ganpati Matoli, betelnut signifies stability and growth. It reflects the cultural heritage of Goa, where betelnut cultivation has been part of daily life for centuries. Its presence in the Matoli highlights both the spiritual value and the close bond Goans share with their agricultural roots.
