Hogplum in Ganpati Matoli

Spiritual Representation

In Goa, the Matoli canopy during Ganesh Chaturthi is decorated with a wide variety of fruits, vegetables, herbs, and plants collected from forests and fields. Among these, the hogplum (locally known as Ambade) holds a special place.

Hogplum is believed to symbolize strength, resilience, and the close bond between humans and nature. Its tangy fruit not only adds flavor to traditional dishes but also represents fertility and nourishment.

The inclusion of hogplum in the Matoli highlights the community’s gratitude towards nature’s bounty. It reflects the traditional Goan way of respecting seasonal produce and celebrating the harmony between festivals and agriculture.
